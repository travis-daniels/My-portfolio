Professional Summary

I am a Systems Administrator with the Charlotte County Board of County Commissioners, where I support and administer enterprise IT infrastructure, Microsoft 365 services, Active Directory, and identity management. My responsibilities include managing user lifecycle processes, troubleshooting infrastructure and messaging issues, administering access controls, and improving operational efficiency through automation.

A significant focus of my work is enterprise automation and systems administration. I develop PowerShell solutions to streamline administrative processes, including user onboarding, offboarding, account management, reporting, and Microsoft 365 administration. I also develop administrative tools and graphical interfaces designed to standardize workflows and reduce repetitive manual tasks.

I have also contributed to the development of Charlotte County's enterprise PRTG Network Operations Center (NOC). This environment monitors approximately 370 servers, 1,200 network devices, and 8,500 sensors. My responsibilities include the organization and structure of monitored equipment, development of custom dashboard views using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG, and the creation of a geographic monitoring map displaying infrastructure locations and device health across the county.

My technical background also includes Microsoft Defender for Office 365, Conditional Access, Exchange Online, endpoint management, infrastructure troubleshooting, and enterprise security practices. I bring additional experience in cloud fundamentals through AWS and Microsoft technologies, supported by industry certifications and hands-on administration.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from Western Governors University, along with industry certifications including CompTIA Security+, Network+, A+, Project+, ITIL Foundation, and AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner.

My continued professional development is focused on enterprise automation, cloud infrastructure, and systems engineering. I plan to pursue a Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Information Technology Management through Western Governors University to further develop my technical, analytical, and business-oriented skills.

I am interested in opportunities that allow me to design, automate, improve, and maintain enterprise technology environments while contributing to scalable and reliable IT solutions.
