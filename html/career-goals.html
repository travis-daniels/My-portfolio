Featured Project: Countywide PRTG Monitoring & NOC Initiative

Organization: Charlotte County Board of County Commissioners

Technologies: PRTG Network Monitor, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG

One of my most significant infrastructure-related projects has been contributing to the organization and development of a countywide PRTG monitoring environment and Network Operations Center (NOC) display system.

The monitoring environment provides visibility into infrastructure health across a large countywide environment, including servers, network equipment, wireless access points, power systems, and environmental monitoring equipment.

Environment Scale

  • Approximately 370 servers.
  • Approximately 1,200 network devices.
  • Approximately 8,500 PRTG monitoring sensors.

My Responsibilities & Contributions

  • Held primary responsibility for organizing and structuring monitored equipment throughout the PRTG environment.
  • Organized monitoring visibility across servers, switches, wireless access points, UPS systems, and environmental controls.
  • Contributed to the development of custom PRTG dashboard views using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG.
  • Helped develop an eight-display NOC monitoring wall using 4K portrait-oriented televisions and custom dashboard layouts.
  • Independently handled the organization and physical location mapping of monitored equipment for a separate geographic PRTG map.
  • Helped establish a dedicated ninth display for the geographic map, providing countywide device location and monitoring status visibility.

Project Outcome

The initiative provides centralized infrastructure visibility through both equipment-focused dashboards and a geographic view of monitored devices throughout the county. The combination of structured monitoring organization, custom visualizations, and geographic mapping helps IT personnel understand infrastructure health and identify where monitored equipment is located.

My primary ownership was the organization and geographic mapping of equipment. Dashboard development and the overall NOC display initiative involved collaborative contributions.
